Interesting Facts About Kazakhstan

Nature of Kazakhstan

Kazakhstan is the ninth largest country in the world. Its territory is larger than Western Europe!

Interesting Facts List

  • Kazakhstan has the second largest oil field in the world - Tengiz
  • Baikonur Cosmodrome is the oldest and largest space launch facility in the world
  • Kumis (fermented mare's milk) is the national drink of Kazakhstan
  • Almaty has the longest passenger tramway (funicular) in the world
